Features

Six small things that make focus stop feeling like punishment

Every one of them is designed from "you'd open this on your own" — not from forced lockouts or anxiety.

Pixel streetscape

Every focus session adds another walker to the street. You can see how many citizens are working alongside you, quietly.

Pomodoro × Pairing

25 / 5 / 50 / 10, your call. Or pair with another focuser and quietly witness each other through one round.

Your room

Every citizen gets a small room. Place a bed, a desk, a wall calendar. Friends can visit but they can only watch — never touch.

lofi, rainfall, café, late-night jazz. Royalty-free library, no ads, no sudden silence, no notifications.

Ranks & achievements

Weekly, monthly, all-time. Unlocked achievements show up as small badges on the street — soft, but every citizen can see.

FAQ

Things you'd ask

Didn't find it? Use the form below — we read every note and reply personally.

Is it really free?
Yes. All core features — streets, focus, pairing, room, shop, music, visitors — are free forever. The future FOCUS+ subscription unlocks only advanced themes and limited cosmetics, never the baseline.
Does it work on mobile?
Works on any desktop browser (Chrome / Safari / Firefox). Mobile web is usable but the experience is tuned for desktop. Native apps aren't on the near-term roadmap.
Can visitors mess with my room?
No. Visitors can only watch and listen — they can't move décor, control music, or talk. The room owner has every control.
Is my data safe?
Email + password sign-up; passwords are bcrypt-hashed (never plaintext). All API traffic is HTTPS-encrypted. Full privacy policy via the footer link.
Can I upload my own music?
For v1, music comes from the built-in library (lofi, jazz, rainfall, royalty-free). Self-upload is on the roadmap once licensing is sorted.
