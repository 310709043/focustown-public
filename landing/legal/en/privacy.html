Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Low Battery Town takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and username (provided at registration).
  • Usage data: Focus session history, accumulated hours, T-coin balance, room configuration, and unlocked achievements.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and page access logs (used for debugging and service improvement).
  • Feedback: Content you voluntarily submit through the feedback form.

2. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not access your camera or microphone.
  • We do not read your screen content or data from other applications.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the core features of the Service.
  • To calculate leaderboards, matching logic, and achievement unlocks.
  • For debugging, security, and service quality improvements.
  • To send you notifications about major updates (only with your consent).

4. Data Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — never as plaintext. All API traffic is encrypted via HTTPS. Our services run on cloud infrastructure with industry-standard security configurations.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts: font loading (no personally identifiable data stored)
  • Cloud infrastructure provider: server hosting and data storage

7. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to maintain your login session. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email us at focustown1314@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 business days.

9. Policy Updates

We'll notify you of material changes to this policy by email or platform announcement.
